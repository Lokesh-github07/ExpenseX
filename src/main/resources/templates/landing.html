About ExpenseX

One place for your everyday money decisions

ExpenseX is a full-stack personal finance application. It was built to show how a secure, database-backed web application can handle authentication, financial records, analytics and reporting end to end.

The problem

Spending is scattered across payments, notes and memory, which makes it hard to see where money goes or whether a budget is holding.

The approach

Record expenses against categories, view totals on a dashboard, and look at trends and reports instead of guessing.

Your data, your account

Every record belongs to your account and is reached only after you sign in with an emailed verification code.
